# ESTABLISHED DESIGN CO. — App Overview & Feature Guide

---

## What It Is

**ESTABLISHED Design Co.** is an all-in-one interior design project management platform built specifically for professional interior designers. It replaces the patchwork of Excel spreadsheets, paper checklists, text threads, and disconnected tools that most designers rely on — and puts everything into one streamlined system that works on desktop, iPad, and mobile.

---

## How It Works

The app has three main interfaces:

1. **Designer Dashboard (Desktop)** — The command center where you manage all projects, spreadsheets, vendors, materials, contacts, and business operations
2. **Mobile App (iPad / iPhone)** — A touch-optimized version that auto-launches on tablets and phones, designed for on-site walkthroughs, photo documentation, punch lists, and offline work at jobsites
3. **Client-Facing Questionnaire** — A branded intake form you send to prospective clients via email. They fill out their design preferences, project details, and book a consultation — all without you lifting a finger

## Core Features

### PROJECT MANAGEMENT
- **Unlimited Projects** — Create and manage multiple design projects simultaneously
- **Project Dashboard** — Every project gets its own hub with quick access to all tools
- **Client Info Tracking** — Client name, contact details, address with Google Maps geolocation, spouse/partner info
- **Project Address Geotagging** — Google Places autocomplete on every address field, linking real-world locations to your projects

### THREE SPREADSHEET SYSTEMS
Each project has three integrated spreadsheets that mirror how designers actually work:

**1. Walkthrough Spreadsheet**
- Room-by-room documentation of every item in the space
- Pre-populated with comprehensive categories: Furniture (Seating, Tables, Storage, Bedroom, Outdoor), Fixtures, Finishes (Paint, Tile, Countertops, Flooring, Hardware), Window Treatments, Accessories, Lighting, Appliances
- Each item tracks: vendor, SKU, price, MSRP, trade discount, size, finish/color, product link, remarks, status, images
- Drag-and-drop reordering
- Auto-syncs to Checklist and FF&E sheets

**2. Checklist Spreadsheet**
- Tracks order status and completion for every item across all rooms
- Status tracking: Not Started, In Progress, Ordered, Shipped, Delivered, Installed, Complete
- Visual status indicators with color coding
- Synced from the Walkthrough — change it once, it updates everywhere

**3. FF&E (Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment) Spreadsheet**
- The specification sheet — formatted for client presentations and vendor orders
- Full product details: vendor, SKU, pricing (trade and retail), dimensions, finish, quantities
- Budget tracking with running totals
- Export-ready for proposals and purchase orders

### CHROME EXTENSION — PRODUCT CLIPPER (Scraper)
- **One-Click Product Scraping** — Browse any vendor website (trade or retail), click the extension, and it automatically pulls: product name, SKU, price, MSRP, size, finish/color, product images, and product URL
- **Editable Fields** — Every scraped field can be edited directly in the side panel before sending to the app
- **Image Gallery** — Detects all product images on the page, lets you select which ones to use
- **Copy Actual Images** — Copies the real product image (not a link) to your clipboard so you can paste directly into Canva or any design tool
- **Send to App** — One click sends all product data directly into your project's spreadsheet
- **Vendor Detection** — Automatically identifies which vendor site you're on
- **Works on Any Vendor Site** — Universal scraper that adapts to different website layouts

### CLIENT QUESTIONNAIRE SYSTEM
- **Branded Email Delivery** — Send a beautifully designed questionnaire email from info@estdesignco.com
- **Comprehensive Intake Form** — Collects: client info, project type (new build, renovation, refresh), budget range, style preferences, room priorities, timeline, design involvement level, material preferences, inspiration uploads
- **Google Maps Address Autocomplete** — Clients type their project address and it auto-completes with geolocation
- **Appointment Booking** — After submitting the questionnaire, clients see available time slots (Tuesday–Friday, 9am–5pm) and book a consultation directly
- **Outlook Calendar Integration** — Booked appointments sync with your Outlook calendar, blocked times automatically removed from availability
- **Edit Mode** — Review and edit submitted questionnaires from inside the app

### MOBILE APP (iPad / iPhone)
- **Auto-Detection** — When opened on a tablet or phone, automatically switches to the mobile-optimized interface
- **Add to Home Screen** — Install as a standalone app from Safari (no App Store needed)
- **Full Project Access** — Open any project, view and edit walkthrough/FF&E/checklist spreadsheets
- **Touch-Optimized** — Large tap targets, swipe navigation, mobile-friendly layouts
- **Offline Mode** — Download all project data before heading to a jobsite. Works without internet. Changes sync automatically when back online
- **Offline Status Indicator** — Always shows whether you're connected or in offline mode
- **Photo Capture** — Take photos on-site and attach them to specific rooms and items
- **Voice Notes** — Record voice memos and attach to rooms or items
- **Punch List Mode** — Walk through a project and log punch list items on the spot
- **Leica D5 Integration** — Connect to Leica Disto D5 laser measurer via Bluetooth for instant room measurements

### CONTACTS MANAGEMENT
- **Master Contact List** — Company-wide contact database shared across all projects
- **Per-Project Contacts** — Assign contacts to specific projects
- **Roles & Categories** — Organize contacts by role: Client, Contractor, Electrician, Plumber, Vendor Rep, Installer, Architect, etc.
- **Autocomplete** — Start typing a name and it suggests from your existing contacts

### VENDOR & MATERIALS MANAGEMENT
- **Vendor Database** — Track all your trade vendor relationships
- **Material Library** — Catalog of materials with specs, images, and vendor associations
- **Product Library** — Save products from the clipper to a reusable library
- **Trade Discount Manager** — Track trade pricing vs. retail pricing per vendor
- **Vendor Portal Integration** — Store login credentials for vendor portals, search product catalogs directly from the app
- **Barcode Scanning** — Scan product barcodes to look up items instantly

### WHOLE HOME FINISHES
- **Finish Tracking** — Track paint colors, tile, countertops, flooring, hardware, and plumbing finishes across the entire home
- **Consistency Check** — See all finish selections in one view to ensure design cohesion

### PHOTOS & DOCUMENTATION
- **Photo Upload** — Upload photos per room and per item
- **Geo-Tagged Photos** — Photos include location data for mapping
- **Room-Based Organization** — Photos organized by room automatically
- **Before/After Tracking** — Document progress throughout the project

### SAMPLE TRACKER
- **Track Physical Samples** — Log fabric swatches, tile samples, paint chips, etc.
- **Status Tracking** — Requested, Received, Approved, Returned
- **Sync to Material Library** — Link samples to your master material catalog

### CALENDAR & SCHEDULING
- **Master Calendar** — Company-wide calendar showing all project events, deadlines, and deliveries
- **Per-Project Calendars** — Each project has its own timeline
- **Installation Calendar** — Track installation dates and contractor schedules
- **Delivery Tracking** — Carrier tracking integration for shipped items
- **Outlook Calendar Sync** — Two-way sync with Microsoft Outlook

### TO-DO & TASK MANAGEMENT
- **Company-Wide To-Do** — Master task list across all projects
- **Per-Project Tasks** — Project-specific task tracking
- **Priority Levels** — High, Medium, Low priority with color coding

### PUNCH LIST

- **Linked to FF&E** — Punch list items link to the original FF&E items
- **Photo Attachments** — Attach photos to punch list items
- **Status Tracking** — Open, In Progress, Complete

### CRITICAL PATH

- **Dependency Tracking** — See what's blocking progress

### SHIPPING & DELIVERY TRACKER
- **Carrier Integration** — Track shipments across carriers

- **Delivery Calendar Sync** — Scheduled deliveries appear on the project calendar

### REPORTS & EXPORTS
- **PDF Report Generation** — Generate professional PDF reports of any project data
- **PDF with Linked Images** — Create PDFs that include product images with clickable links
- **Project Export** — Export full project data
- **Budget Reports** — Financial summaries with trade vs. retail pricing

### CALCULATORS
- **Built-in Calculators** — Square footage, material quantity, pricing, measurement conversions
- **Accessible from Any Screen** — Quick calculator popup available everywhere

### DESIGN TOOLS
- **Furniture Layout Planner** — Visual room layout tool
- **Color Palette Extractor** — Extract color palettes from images
- **AR Furniture Preview** — Augmented reality preview for furniture placement
- **Lighting Simulator** — Simulate different lighting scenarios
- **Room Scanner 3D** — 3D room scanning integration
- **Pinterest Integration** — Pull inspiration directly from Pinterest boards

### AI ASSISTANT
- **AI-Powered Design Help** — Ask questions about materials, design choices, and get intelligent suggestions
- **Smart Alternatives** — Get AI-suggested alternative products when items are out of stock or over budget

### CANVA INTEGRATION
- **Board Import** — Import product data from Canva design boards
- **Image Upload** — Upload room and item images to Canva

### DATA SECURITY & BACKUP
- **Full Backup** — Download complete database backup (projects, contacts, materials)
- **Selective Backup** — Backup just contacts, materials, or projects separately
- **Restore** — Restore from any backup file
- **Offline Data Persistence** — All data cached locally for offline access

## Workflow: How Designers Use It

1. **Client Inquiry** → Send the branded questionnaire email
2. **Client Fills Out** → They submit preferences, address (geo-tagged), and book a consultation
3. **Create Project** → Project auto-created from questionnaire data
4. **Initial Walkthrough** → Take iPad to the property, use the mobile app for room-by-room documentation
5. **Product Sourcing** → Browse vendor sites, use the Chrome Extension to clip products directly into the project
6. **Specification** → Walkthrough, Checklist, and FF&E spreadsheets auto-populate and stay in sync
7. **Client Presentation** → Export professional PDFs and reports
8. **Order Management** → Track orders, deliveries, and installations via the Checklist and Shipping Tracker
9. **Installation** → Use Punch List mode on-site to document issues
10. **Project Completion** → Final walkthrough, close out project

## Technical Details

- **Web App**: React + Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Python FastAPI + MongoDB
- **Mobile**: Progressive Web App (PWA) — installable from Safari, works offline
- **Chrome Extension**: Manifest V3, works on any vendor website
- **Email**: Microsoft 365 SMTP integration (info@estdesignco.com)
- **Calendar**: Outlook Calendar API integration
- **Maps**: Google Places API for address autocomplete and geolocation
- **Hosting**: Cloud-deployed at app.estdesignco.com
